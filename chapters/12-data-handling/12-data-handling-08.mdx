## 12.8 Analysing data (EMG7C)

The analysis of data is a continuous process that starts with the gathering of information. Analysis is best described as looking at information carefully and making a decision. After we have calculated the measures of central tendency and spread; and represented our data graphically, we can then comment on whether there are any trends or patterns in the data.
